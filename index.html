Round 4 — Error detective

Find the incorrect part of each sentence. 15 points each.

Teacher answer key and explanations
  1. Doctors should explain the risks before treatment. — After should use the base verb.
  2. The results were influenced by patient expectations. — Plural results takes were.
  3. If I were a doctor, I would explain the treatment. — Were is preferred in formal hypothetical conditionals.
  4. This treatment is safer than the other one. — Do not combine more with safer.
  5. They conducted the experiment in 1996. — Use past simple with finished past time.
  6. The research might explain why some patients feel better. — Use the base verb after might.
